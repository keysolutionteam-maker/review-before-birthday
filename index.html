How it works
  1. Every Monday: export your client and policy list from the company system as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, then tap Import client list.
  2. Check the column matching once. The app remembers it for next week.
  3. To review and invite: clients whose birthday is about 2 months away. Look over their policies, choose English or Malay, edit the message if you like, then tap Send on WhatsApp. You press Send in WhatsApp yourself, then tell the app you sent it.
  4. When the client replies, enter the appointment date and time. The app lines up a reminder for 3 days before.
  5. Reminders to send: send these on the day shown. After the meeting, tap Mark review done.
  6. Needs checking: clients with a missing or conflicting birth date, IC or phone. The app never guesses these. Fix them in the company system and re-import.
  7. Download this week's PDF for the weekly list, plus the KST review pack for each client: Total Protection Planning Record 全方面保障记录, 全方面保障计划, Wealth Protection (5 Fingers) and 资产/负债/现金流. Their name, policies, year issued, premiums and beneficiaries are filled in. The coverage boxes are left for you to fill in at the meeting.

Your client list stays in this browser on this device. Only your progress (invited, appointment, reminder sent) is saved to your account, under a coded ID with no names.
